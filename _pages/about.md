First-year PhD candidate at HKUST NLP Group. Research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, and research interests include LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning, Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM), and LLM truthfulness and Interpretability.

## Personal Details

- **Position:** First-year PhD candidate at HKUST NLP Group; Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024-Present) at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.
