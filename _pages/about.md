Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate in the **HKUST NLP Group** at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He. He graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with research interests in LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs), and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

## Skills & Research Interests

- Natural language processing
- Machine learning
- LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning
- Hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs)
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability
